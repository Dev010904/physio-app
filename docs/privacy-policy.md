PhysioSathi helps you do the home exercises your physiotherapist gives you.
It is made by [developer or company name] ("we"). This policy explains what
the app keeps on your phone, what it sends, who can see it and how to delete it.

## The short version

- Without a clinic code, everything you do stays on your phone.
- If you link the app to your physio with a code, your exercise results are
  sent to your physio's clinic so they can follow your progress.
- The camera only counts your reps. The picture never leaves your phone and
  is never recorded.
- Private notes stay on your phone unless you choose "Share with my physio".
- You can delete your data at any time in **Settings → Your data → Delete my data**.

## What stays on your phone

- Your exercise history: the sessions you did, sets and reps, pain scores (0 to 10) and how each session felt.
- Notes you write after an exercise.
- Your reminder time, language and appearance settings.

None of this is sent anywhere unless you link the app to a clinic.

## What is sent when you link to your physio

Linking is optional. You do it by entering the code your physio gives you.
The app then creates an anonymous account for your phone. It has no email,
phone number or password, only a random ID. Your clinic has already added
your name and language when it made the code.

After linking, the app sends the following for sessions of your physio's program:

- The date and time of each session, and whether it felt easy, OK or hard.
- For each exercise: sets and reps done, whether the reps were counted by the timer or the camera, the best movement in degrees (when the camera counted), and your pain score.
- Your note, but only if you turned on "Share with my physio" for it.
- Safety alerts: when you report pain of 8 or more, chest pain, numbness, sudden severe pain or a fall, the app tells your physio, along with the exercise you were doing.

It also downloads the exercise program your physio made for you.

**Who can see it:** only the physiotherapists at your clinic. Other clinics
cannot see it. The database enforces this, and we test that it does. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

**Where it is stored:** with our hosting provider Supabase, on servers in
Mumbai, India. All data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Like most online services,
the hosting provider keeps short technical logs, such as IP addresses, for
security.

## Camera

If you turn on "Count with camera", the app uses the front camera to see your
body position and count your reps. The pictures are processed on your phone
as they arrive and thrown away straight after. They are never saved or sent.
Only the rep count and best movement angle are kept. The app does not use the
microphone.

## Crash reports

When the app crashes or has an error, it sends a crash report to Sentry, a
service that helps us fix bugs. A report holds the error details, the phone
model, Android version and app version, and a random installation ID. Reports
are set not to include your name, your exercise data, your IP address or
screenshots. A sample of reports also measures how fast screens load. Sentry
stores reports in the United States.

## Other things the app uses

- **Voice:** spoken exercise cues use your phone's own text-to-speech.
- **Reminders:** daily reminders are scheduled on your phone; no server is involved.

## How long we keep it

Data on your phone stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. Data at your
clinic stays until you delete it, or your clinic removes you as a patient.

## Deleting your data

- **In the app:** Settings → Your data → Delete my data. If you are linked,
  this first deletes your record at the clinic, including all your sessions,
  pain scores, shared notes and alerts. It then deletes everything on your
  phone. It needs the internet. If you are offline, nothing is deleted and the
  app asks you to try again.
- **Ask us:** write to [contact email] and we will delete your data.
- Uninstalling the app deletes the data on your phone, but not your record at the clinic.

## Children

PhysioSathi is meant for adults, or for children using it with a parent or
guardian under a physiotherapist's care. If you are under 18, a parent or
guardian should agree to linking the app with a clinic.

## Not a medical device

PhysioSathi helps you follow your physiotherapist's advice. It does not
diagnose or treat any condition. If something feels wrong, stop and contact
your physio. In an emergency, call 112.
